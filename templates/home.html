How it works

From surplus to shared meal,
in four clear steps.

Food Bridge gives every donation a visible, accountable journey — from the donor's kitchen to the NGO's table.

01

Donors share surplus

Restaurants, hotels, event organisers, and individuals list surplus food with quantity, preparation time, and a safe expiry window.

02

NGOs find a match

Verified NGOs see available food ranked by their category preferences, location, quantity requirements, and urgency.

03

Volunteers deliver

Once a request is accepted, an available volunteer is assigned, collects the food, and updates pickup and delivery status.

04

Impact is tracked

Every step is logged. Donors, NGOs, volunteers, and administrators see real, database-backed impact — not estimates.

Built for real operations

More than a donation form.

A complete operating system for food rescue — designed around safety, accountability, and speed.

Smart Food Matching

A transparent, rule-based score ranks donations for each NGO using category, city, quantity, and expiry urgency. Every recommendation explains itself.

Expiry Monitoring

Live countdowns, warning badges, automatic expiry, and a safety rule that blocks expired food from ever being requested or picked up.

Journey Tracking

A seven-stage progress tracker follows each donation from creation to completion, with a full event timeline and live status polling.

Automated Notifications

Request updates, volunteer assignments, pickups, deliveries, verification decisions, and expiry warnings arrive in-app for the right people.

No missed handoffs

Impact Analytics

Category breakdowns, monthly trends, rescued quantities, connected NGOs, and active volunteer counts — all calculated from live records.

Database-backed

Roles & Governance

Separate workspaces for donors, NGOs, volunteers, and administrators, with NGO verification, account blocking, and an audit trail.

About Food Bridge

We believe surplus food is not waste. It is an opportunity.

Food Bridge is a social-impact platform that helps restaurants, hotels, event organisers, and individuals share surplus food with verified NGOs. Volunteers coordinate the pickup and delivery, while the platform keeps every handoff transparent.

Our mission is simple: make food rescue safe, fast, and measurable. We do that by combining a clear donation workflow, expiry monitoring, smart matching, and impact analytics in one place.

Dignity for every meal Food reaches community organisations who know their people best.
Safety without friction Clear expiry rules protect recipients while keeping donations easy.
Technology that disappears Simple dashboards for the people doing the real work.
